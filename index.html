A TINY TASTE OF THE LAB

Meet your first
DNA data point.

DNA is biological information written with four letters: A, T, C, and G. A computer can count those letters in seconds — even in very long sequences.

i

What is GC content?
It is the percentage of bases in a sequence that are G or C. It describes a sequence; by itself, it does not tell you what an organism is or whether a gene is active.

This demo uses a short example sequence for learning, not for clinical or diagnostic use.
